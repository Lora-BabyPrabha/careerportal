Senior Frontend Developer

Location: Remote | Experience: 5+ Years | Type: Full-time

Job Description

We are looking for a Senior Frontend Developer to join our team. You will be responsible for building modern, responsive web applications using React.js, TypeScript, and other frontend technologies.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
  • Strong knowledge in React.js, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Experience with REST APIs and UI/UX best practices
  • Ability to work collaboratively with design and backend teams

Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain reusable components
  • Work closely with designers and backend developers
  • Ensure responsive and cross-browser compatibility
  • Participate in code reviews and team discussions

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and bonus
  • Flexible work hours and remote options
  • Professional development allowance
  • Health insurance and wellness programs
  • Paid time off and holidays
